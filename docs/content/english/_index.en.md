This encyclopedia presents a systematic overview of dyslexia based on the research of Dr. Sally Shaywitz at Yale University. It covers the nature of dyslexia, how it is diagnosed, evidence-based interventions, school selection, college success, and the full range of supports available to dyslexic individuals of all ages.

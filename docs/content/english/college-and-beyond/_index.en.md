Dyslexia does not end at high school graduation. College introduces new challenges — greater reading volume, higher-stakes timed assessments, and less structured support — that can expose previously compensated dyslexic individuals. At the same time, higher education and adulthood also bring new tools, legal protections, and strategies for success. With the right preparation and support, dyslexic individuals thrive in college and build rewarding careers.

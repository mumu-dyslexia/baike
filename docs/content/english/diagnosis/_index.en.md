Accurate diagnosis is the gateway to effective support. Without a formal evaluation, a struggling reader may spend years being blamed for not trying hard enough, losing confidence and falling further behind. With a diagnosis, the same child gains access to evidence-based intervention, legal protections, and — perhaps most importantly — a truthful explanation for why reading is hard.

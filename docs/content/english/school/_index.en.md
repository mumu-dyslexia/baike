Where a dyslexic child goes to school can make a substantial difference in their reading development, confidence, and overall educational experience. Not all schools are equally prepared to support students with phonological reading difficulties. Understanding what to look for — and what questions to ask — allows families to make informed choices and advocate effectively wherever their child is enrolled.

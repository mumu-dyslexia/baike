Supporting a dyslexic individual goes beyond reading instruction. Dyslexia intersects with emotional well-being, frequently co-occurs with other conditions like anxiety and ADHD, and carries legal dimensions that families and individuals should understand. Technology has also transformed what is possible for dyslexic readers at every age. This section covers the full range of supports available.
